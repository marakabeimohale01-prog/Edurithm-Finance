Privacy & Data Protection Policy

How Edurithm Finance collects, uses, and protects the personal information of applicants, borrowers, and website users.

Last reviewed: 2026 · This policy applies to Edurithm Finance's website, WhatsApp, email, and telephone communications.

1. Who We Are

Edurithm Finance ("Edurithm") is a student-focused financial business operating in Lesotho. The registered legal name, registration details, address and official privacy contact are to be confirmed.

2. Scope

This policy explains how Edurithm processes personal information relating to applicants, borrowers, website users and persons communicating with Edurithm.

3. Information We Collect

We may collect identity and contact information, student and enrolment information, sponsorship or funding information, application and loan information, payment or account information, communications, verification information, and technical information generated through website use.

4. How We Collect Information

Information may be collected through website forms, WhatsApp, email, telephone, documents supplied by applicants, and lawful verification channels.

5. Purposes

We process information for applications, identity and eligibility and affordability assessment, loan administration, communication, fraud prevention, compliance, lawful recovery, complaints, record keeping, security and other disclosed lawful purposes.

6. Lawful Processing and Minimisation

Edurithm processes personal information only on a lawful basis and limits collection to information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

7. Credit and NMDS Information

We may process loan, repayment, student, sponsorship/funding and NMDS-contract information. Credit information may be obtained from, or supplied to, credit-reporting bodies where permitted or required by law.

8. Sharing

Information may be shared with authorised personnel, service providers, professional advisers, auditors, regulators, courts, law-enforcement bodies and credit-reporting bodies where lawful.

9. Third-Party and International Processing

Where providers such as hosting, form-processing, payment or communications services process information on our behalf, Edurithm will assess appropriate privacy and security arrangements. International processing will be handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

10. Security

Edurithm applies reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including access controls, authentication, secure transmission, confidentiality obligations, device security, backups and incident-response procedures.

11. Data Breaches

Edurithm will assess, contain and remediate personal-data incidents and notify affected persons or authorities where required by law.

12. Retention

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for lawful purposes, contractual administration, dispute resolution, fraud prevention and record keeping, except where a longer period is required by law or regulation. Required retention periods prevail.

13. Individual Rights

Subject to applicable law and lawful exemptions, individuals may have rights including access to, and correction of, their personal information. Identity may be verified before any request is actioned.

14. Direct Communications

Edurithm may use supplied contact details for applications, account administration, notices and support. Any marketing communications will comply with applicable law and consent/opt-out requirements.

15. Cookies

The website may use necessary cookies. Where non-essential analytics or tracking is introduced, appropriate notice and consent will be provided where required.

16. Children

Edurithm's lending products are intended for persons aged 18 and above. Edurithm does not knowingly process loan applications from persons under 18.

17. Privacy Complaints

Privacy concerns may be raised with the Manager at Edurithm Finance by direct call on 58036786. Edurithm will record, investigate and respond through its internal process.

18. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when services, technology, processing practices or legal obligations change. Material changes will be communicated where required.
